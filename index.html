Bulk trading and arbitrage

Moving produce between markets

We intend to trade beans in bulk and capture price differences between markets.

Arbitrage here means buying produce where supply is plentiful and selling it where demand is stronger, once the cost of storage and transport is covered. It rewards knowing both markets better than the next trader.

No trading volumes or results are reported, because trading has not begun.

Operating principles

Principles the venture follows

The venture follows the principles that guide the whole BlackVault group.

Research-driven

Conviction is earned through work. We prefer primary evidence and first-hand understanding to consensus.

Independent judgement

We form our own view and are prepared to hold it, or abandon it, when the evidence changes.

Risk awareness

We start by asking what could go wrong, how badly, and whether we could withstand it.

Disciplined execution

A sound idea is only the beginning. Value is made in the details of delivery, governance and follow-through.

Long-term value creation

We measure success across cycles and decades, and avoid decisions that borrow from the future.
